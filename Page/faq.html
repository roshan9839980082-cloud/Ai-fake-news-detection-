Everything you need to know about NewsGuard AI

How does NewsGuard AI detect fake news?

NewsGuard AI uses advanced Google Gemini AI to analyze news content. It checks the text for factual accuracy, cross-references claims with known verified sources, analyzes the language for emotional manipulation, and provides a confidence score along with a detailed explanation.

Is my data private?

Yes, your data is completely private. Your news checks and saved articles are only accessible to you. We use Supabase with Row Level Security to ensure your data is protected.

What types of content can I analyze?

You can analyze three types of content: (1) Article Text — paste any news article text, (2) Image URL — provide a link to a news image, and (3) Article URL — provide a direct link to a news article.

How accurate is the AI analysis?

Our AI achieves approximately 98.7% accuracy on verified test datasets. However, AI is not perfect and we always recommend cross-checking important news with multiple reputable sources.

Can I save articles for later?

Yes! After analyzing any article, you can save it to your Saved Articles collection. You can also save articles directly from the Recent True News section.

Is NewsGuard AI free to use?

NewsGuard AI offers a free tier with basic features. Upgrade to Pro to unlock advanced analysis reports, unlimited checks, priority processing, and detailed source verification.

How do I report an incorrect result?

If you believe the AI has made an incorrect assessment, you can use the Contact page to report it. Our team reviews flagged results regularly to improve accuracy.

Does NewsGuard work in multiple languages?

Currently NewsGuard AI is optimized for English content. Support for additional languages including Hindi, Spanish, French, and German is coming soon.
