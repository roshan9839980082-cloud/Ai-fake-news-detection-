How It Works

Input Your Content

Article URL

Share a link to any news article. We analyze the URL structure, domain reputation, and content credibility.

Understanding Verdicts

What each verdict means

True

Content is factually accurate and sourced from credible, verified sources.

False

Content contains verifiable falsehoods, fabricated information, or deliberate misinformation.

Misleading

Content contains elements of truth but is presented in a way that misleads the reader.

Unverified

Content cannot be verified with currently available information and sources.
